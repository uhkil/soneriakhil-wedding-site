# Drawing masters

The original drawings, as SVG files, already colored in the site's dark blue
(#003459) with their light areas cut out so they're see-through. Edit these.

The site itself shows WebP images rendered from them (in `public/assets/art/`
and `public/assets/hero/`), about 1600 pixels on the long side. SVGs that use
cut-outs are drawn blurry by Safari on iPhones; the WebP images stay sharp
everywhere and are smaller to download.

After changing a drawing here, re-render its WebP (ask Claude to "re-render
the drawings").
